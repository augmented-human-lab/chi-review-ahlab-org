Recommendation scale used by the reviewer
A accept with minor revisions · ARR between A and RR · RR revise and resubmit · RRX between RR and reject · X reject. Papers are scored on originality, correctness, novelty, importance and clarity of exposition. Roughly 75% of submissions are rejected.
